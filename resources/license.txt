1. What DeskNotch does
DeskNotch shows a notch at the top of your screen with music controls, tasks, reminders, a focus timer, files, screen time and AI usage limits.

2. Data on your PC
Your tasks, settings, reminders and pinned files are stored only on this PC, in DeskNotch's own data folder. DeskNotch has no account and no server of its own.

3. Other services
DeskNotch can show your Claude Code and Codex plan limits. Before it does, it asks for your permission. With your permission, it reads the sign-in those tools already keep on this PC and sends it only to their own provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) to fetch your usage. It is not stored elsewhere or sent anywhere else. You can withdraw permission at any time by switching AI usage off in Settings.
Your use of Claude Code, Codex, ScreenWise or any other service stays under that service's own terms. DeskNotch is not made, endorsed or supported by Anthropic, OpenAI or any of them.
If ScreenWise is installed, DeskNotch reads its local database, read-only, to show today's screen time.
